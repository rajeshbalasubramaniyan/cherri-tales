From vine to jar

The long, slow Malnad way

Malnad is the rainforest belt of Karnataka's Western Ghats. Heavy monsoon rain and shade-grown farms give these spices their strong aroma. We keep the rest simple.

  1. 🌧️

    Grown in the rain

    Pepper vines climb shade trees through months of monsoon. No hurry.

  2. 🧺

    Picked by hand

    Harvested when ripe, cluster by cluster, by people who know each vine.

  3. ☀️

    Sun-dried

    Spread on mats under the winter sun until they're crackling dry.

  4. 🫙

    Packed fresh

    Cleaned, sorted and packed in small batches, then sent to you.
